Less Hard Drop, More Soft Bop

The goal of this R&D initiative was to merge classic Tetris mechanics with the juicy, tactile elements of modern casual games.

This brief triggered a complete “jellification” of all game elements.

But how much jelly is enough?

The Rules of the Jiggle

Maintaining tactile satisfaction comes down to context and material integrity.

To find the sweet spot, I created two distinct sets of animation rules:

Material Integrity
We maintained the core identity of the physical objects, ensuring structures kept their weight and friction.

Maximum Tactility
UI elements squash, stretch, and pop back into place. Menus and pop-ups spring onto the screen with playful elasticity.

Be There and Be Square

A meta-layer only works if the world feels alive. Alongside Terry and dog Cube, you rebuild the Blockies’ world by earning resources. The player’s challenge is the grid but ours was bringing the Blockies to life.

We built the Blockies as modular characters from the same square DNA as the game pieces with same geometry but entirely different personality.

The 45-Degree

We built our particle system using the exact DNA of the game: the square mino. By simply rotating the classic square 45 degrees, we transformed it into a diamond.

A flat square is a heavy, rigid brick, but a rotated square is a dynamic, lightweight sparkle.

De-risking the Production

My approach focused on validating ideas early, reducing technical uncertainty, and keeping production moving in parallel.

Proxy-first workflow
I built a proxy pipeline to validate animation timing before final assets existed, cutting delivery time by 40%.

Prototype before it costs you
By prototyping in After Effects before engine implementation, we aligned stakeholders early and reduced technical rework by 80%.

Success in the Failure

This was my biggest project and my biggest failure.

Even though the game was withdrawn from the App Store, the R&D wasn’t wasted. Managing an international team and cross-departmental workflows at this scale became a masterclass in leadership.

I learned I can control the quality and efficiency of the pipeline but not the market.

Some projects ship to millions, others never launch. The discipline and leadership I built here have been more valuable than most measurable wins.
